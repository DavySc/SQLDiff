Convert and compare

  
Convert rewrites the left side into the target dialect; each converted statement gets a badge, and only converted means no assumption was needed and SQLDiff found no difference. A target without rules yet shows every statement as not converted. Compare diffs the left side against the right one (both editable; the right starts as the conversion), as sqldiff diff does. Pick the dialects at the top at any time.

  

    
 convertedRules without assumptions; the engine found nothing.

    
 under assumptionsEqual if the listed facts hold.

    
 needs a decisionSeveral faithful targets exist; check the note.

    
 differsThe engine found a difference or leftover T-SQL.

    
 not convertedNo rule yet; kept as a comment.

Hover, clickHovering a statement on either side shows its counterpart, joined by a band in the gutter. Clicking keeps the side you clicked still and brings the counterpart level with it. A dashed outline marks the other part of a statement the conversion split, such as a DECLARE moved to the declare section.

    
Compare coloursAs in WinMerge: yellow lines changed (the changed words darker), red only on the left, green only on the right; a thin rule marks where the other side has lines this one lacks. This is a text diff that follows your typing; SQLDiff's findings are listed below. The caret's changed line and its counterpart are outlined, and a click brings them level.
