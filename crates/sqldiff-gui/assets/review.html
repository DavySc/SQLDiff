Reviewing a converted tree

The queue lists what a person should look at, worst first, by tier (never by a score). Each decision you make is appended to review-decisions.jsonl in the converted tree, with your name, your reason and the hashes of both files; the converted files are never changed. After every decision the sources are read again, the ledger applied again and the queue rebuilt.

AcceptAn assumption holds here. Bulk accept records one decision per selected unit.
PickThe target the conversion chose is the right one.
ApproveThe output is right as written. Needed for output an agent wrote and for plainly converted units. Never discharges a difference, a refusal or what the engine could not prove, and does not accept assumptions.
RejectThe unit is wrong and must be fixed; it stays open, whoever approved it.
CommentA remark only; it discharges nothing.

accepted in review means people accepted everything that was open; it is never the same as converted. Nothing found, not proven is a plainly converted unit: the rules needed no assumption and SQLDiff found no difference, which is not a proof. A decision made on other file contents is outdated and must be confirmed again.
